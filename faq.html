FAQ & Support

Answers to common questions about using this campus marketplace prototype.

How do I post an item?

On the home page, choose Post an item, complete the simulated email verification, and add a photo and item details. Posts are saved in your browser only; they are not published to a real marketplace.

How do I contact a seller?

Open an item and select Message seller. The prototype asks for simulated verification and lets you type a message, but it does not deliver messages to real sellers.

Where should I meet the seller?

Choose a public, well-lit place on campus, such as a busy student center or library entrance. The listed meetup spot is only a suggestion in this demo.

What if my item does not sell?

In the wider home-page layout, My listings includes a Donate / recycle option for your own items. That control is hidden on small screens. It only records your choice in this browser; it does not arrange a handoff. Check your campus community center or student resource hub for donation options, or follow campus recycling guidance.

How can I use the site with a keyboard?

Use Tab or Shift + Tab to move between controls. Enter activates links, and Enter or Space activates buttons and focused marketplace listings. The FAQ questions can be opened or closed with Enter or Space. On the home page, ⌘ K (Mac) or Ctrl + K focuses search, and Escape closes an open dialog.
