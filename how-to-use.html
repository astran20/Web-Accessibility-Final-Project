A QUICK GUIDE TO CAMPUS MARKET

How to use Campus Market

Find useful things nearby, pass along what you no longer need, and keep every handoff thoughtful. This is a front-end course prototype, so some actions are demonstrations rather than live services.

01 / EXPLORE

Browse & Search

Use the search box to find listings by item name, category, description, or campus location. Choose a category to narrow the results, then select a listing to see its price, condition, description, seller, and meetup spot. You can also sort by newest or price.

02 / PASS IT ON

Post an Item

Select Post an item on the home page and complete the simulated school-email verification. Add a photo (JPG, PNG, or WEBP up to 5 MB), category, price, condition, meetup spot, and description. Your listing is saved only in this browser; this prototype does not publish it to a campus-wide service.

03 / MAKE A PLAN

Contact & Meet

Open a listing and choose Message seller. The demo asks you to complete simulated verification first. Messages are only simulated in this prototype, not delivered to a real seller. If arranging a real handoff separately, meet in a public, well-lit place on campus.

04 / KEEP IT CIRCULATING

Donate or Recycle

In the wider home-page layout, open My listings for one of your own items and choose Donate / recycle to mark it for either option. That control is hidden on small screens. The prototype only saves the choice in this browser; it does not arrange a donation or recycling pickup. Check your campus community center or student resource hub for donation drop-offs, and follow your campus waste program's recycling guidance.

05 / KNOW WHAT'S A DEMO

Prototype limits

Account verification accepts a demo email and any six-digit code. New listings, favorites, and listing changes use this browser's local storage. Posting, messaging, verification, reporting, and donation or recycling are not connected to real services.

Keyboard Help

Use Tab and Shift + Tab to move through links and controls, then press Enter to follow a link or activate a focused listing, and Space to activate a focused button or listing. On the home page, press ⌘ K (Mac) or Ctrl + K (other keyboards) to focus search. Press Escape to close an open home-page dialog. On the FAQ page, focus a question and press Enter or Space to open or close it.
