About Campus Market

Campus Market is a secondhand marketplace for students. It helps students save money, reuse useful items, and create less waste together.

01 / OUR COMMUNITY

About Campus Market

Students can browse useful items offered by other students and pass along things they no longer need. Keeping these exchanges close to campus can make everyday essentials more affordable and give good items a second life.

02 / WHO IT IS FOR

Who Is It For?

Campus Market is designed for students who want affordable books, clothing, electronics, and home goods, and for students who want to find a new home for items they have finished using. The campus setting encourages nearby, student-to-student exchanges.

03 / WHAT YOU CAN DO

Main Features

Browse sample listings, search and filter by category, sort by price or date, view item details, save favorites in this browser, and try posting or messaging in a local demo. The sample uses simulated email verification; it does not connect students or publish listings to a real marketplace.

Real student accounts, live listings shared across devices, delivered messages, and campus donation partnerships are planned features. They are not available in this prototype.

04 / A SIMPLE EXCHANGE

How It Works

Browse or search for an item, choose a listing to read its details, and check its suggested campus meetup spot. In this demo, you can try the post and message steps after simulated verification; those actions are not connected to real students. Arrange any real exchange separately and meet in a public place.

05 / LESS WASTE, MORE USE

Sustainability

Buying and passing on secondhand items can keep useful things in use and reduce avoidable waste. When an item is not sold, its owner can look for a local donation option or follow campus recycling guidance. The demo can record a donation or recycling choice for your listing, but it does not arrange a real drop-off or collection.
